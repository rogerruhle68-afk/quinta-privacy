Quinta Duel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Quinta Duel lets you play Quinta online with friends. It needs no account, email or password. To run games between phones, the name you choose and your games are kept on the Quinta Duel server (Google Firebase). The free version shows ads from Google AdMob.

What the server keeps

The first time you open the app, Google Firebase gives it a random player ID. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple / Google account. Using that ID, the Quinta Duel server keeps:

  • the display name you choose (checked so it isn't offensive);
  • your games: scorecards, dice and moves, whose turn it is, and the result;
  • your win / loss / tie record against each friend you've played;
  • anyone you have blocked;
  • invite codes you create (they expire after 7 days);
  • a push-notification token, so the server can tell your phone when it's your turn.

Your friends see your display name, your scorecards and your moves in games with them. Nobody else can see your games. The dice are rolled on the server so nobody can cheat.

Reports

If you report another player's name, we receive that name, your report and both player IDs, so we can review it and change an offensive name.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software may collect your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), device identifiers including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking, which ads you see and tap, and app performance and crash information. The developer does not receive this information. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Notifications: you can turn off Quinta Duel notifications at any time in your phone's settings. You can still play; you just won't be told when it's your turn.
  • Tracking (iPhone and iPad): iOS asks whether the app may track you. You can say no and still play, and change it later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android): you can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Consent: where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice under Settings & Rules > Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Block and report: you can block a player so they can't start new games with you, and report an offensive name.
  • Remove ads: a one-time in-app purchase turns the ads off.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple or Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.

What the app does not do

  • It has no chat and no way to message other players.
  • It does not match you with strangers. You only play people you invite or who invite you.
  • It includes no analytics tools of its own.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Third parties

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging) to run games, and Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform SDK for ads. Firebase stores game data in the United States. See Firebase privacy and security.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings & Rules > Delete My Data resigns your games in progress and removes your name, records, blocks and player ID from the server right away. Finished games stay with the friends who played them, with your name replaced by "Former player". You can also email support@quintadice.com to ask us to delete your data. Deleting the app alone does not remove your data from the server.

Children

Quinta Duel is a general-audience dice game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated first, and significant changes will be described in the app's release notes.
