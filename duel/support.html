Common questions

How do I start a game with a friend?

Tap Invite a Friend, pick 3 or 6 columns, and send the link or 6-letter code by text, email or any app. When your friend accepts, the game appears in your list and you go first. Invites last 7 days.

A friend sent me a code. Where do I enter it?

Tap Enter a Code on the home screen. Or just tap the link they sent you.

Can I play someone with a different kind of phone?

Yes. iPhone and Android players can play each other.

How do turns work?

You play 3 turns, then your friend plays 3. Play whenever it suits you; you'll get a notification when it's your turn. A 3-column game is 39 turns each, a 6-column game 78.

My friend stopped playing.

After 3 days without playing, your friend gets a reminder. After 4 days, you can end the game and take the win from the game screen.

I'm not getting "your turn" notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for Quinta Duel in your phone's settings. Your games always update when you open the app.

Someone has an offensive name.

Open a game with them, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Report. You can also Block them so they can't start new games with you.

How do I change my name or delete my data?

Tap Settings & Rules (the gear) and choose Your Name or Delete My Data.

How do I remove the ads?

Tap Settings & Rules > Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase. If you already bought it, tap Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

On iPhone and iPad, through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, through Google Play.
